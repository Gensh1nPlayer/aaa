❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions that most buyers ask before ordering.

Are the accounts safe?

Every account comes from a real player and has a normal play history—not a scripted, botted, studio-farmed, or mass-produced account. We exclude accounts with known histories of cheating, abuse, or other rule violations. Key listing details can be cross-checked against official in-game and Battle.net records during verification.

How fast is delivery?

Most orders are delivered within 5 minutes after payment. Rare top-tier accounts may require additional preparation time.

Payment methods supported?

Credit card, PayPal, crypto, and selected gift cards. Confirm availability and fees on Discord.

How often is the stock updated?

Sales status is updated in real time as accounts are sold, while new stock is typically added every 7 days. The countdown above shows the next planned stock update.

How to use the Battle.net Authenticator?

roshine.love/auth is a private tool for Battle.net Authenticator. Paste the Private Key from the delivered account information, then copy the current code into the Battle.net app when verification is requested.

📌 Authenticator notice

  • Codes refresh every 30 seconds.
  • Each code can be used only once.
  • If a code expires, wait for the next code and try again.
How do I log in to my Overwatch account?

Use the Battle.net credentials supplied with the account and complete Authenticator verification only when the app requests it.

🎮 Overwatch account login

  1. Open the Battle.net Desktop App.
  2. Sign in with the provided Battle.net credentials.
  3. Complete verification with the current Authenticator code if requested.
How do I access the account email?

Use the email information supplied with the account. The exact verification method depends on whether an authenticator is attached.

✉️ Account email access

  • Sign in to live.com with the provided email details.
  • If an authenticator is attached, use Google Authenticator and the provided QR code; otherwise sign in with the email and password.
  • After access, add your own recovery email for better security.
When should I update the account security information?

Keep the same device and a stable IP, and wait about 7 days before changing the password, recovery email, region or other security settings. This reduces security locks and verification issues but cannot eliminate platform risk.

How do I keep the account secure?

Save the delivered account information immediately and protect every login and recovery channel.

🔐 Account security recommendations

  • Keep the email, authenticator and recovery details secure.
  • Avoid public or unstable IP addresses, VPNs, proxies and shared networks.
  • Never share the account information with others.
